TV diary · Android

A well-kept notebook for television.

Log the episodes you watch, rate shows, and see what to start next. Quiet by design, so the posters do the talking.

Download APK ↓ Open the web app →

Version 1.0 · 3 MB · Android 7.0 or newer

See it on a phone

What it does

  • Log in one tap

    Mark an episode watched and move on. Logging is the main action on every screen.

  • Discover what's next

    Browse shows by year and category, and pick the next thing worth starting.

  • Watch with friends

    Stay loosely connected to what friends are watching, without the noise of a social feed.

Install on your phone

  1. 01

    Download Rerun.apk on your Android phone.

  2. 02

    Open the file. If Android asks, allow installs from this source for your browser or files app.

  3. 03

    Tap Install, then open Rerun and sign in or create an account.

The app is signed but not on Google Play, so Android shows an "unknown source" warning. It needs an internet connection.
