Privacy

Rerun stores the information you give it so the app can work: your account email, your profile, the shows you log and rate, and your friend connections.

Where data lives

Account and diary data is stored with Supabase. Show posters and metadata come from TMDB. Sign-in with Google is handled by Google and Supabase.

What I don't do

I don't sell your data or run ads. The app doesn't request camera, microphone, location or contacts permissions.

Deleting your data

Email gaylemonique21@gmail.com and I'll remove your account and diary entries.
